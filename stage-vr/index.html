Placing it, from inside

A ring sits at the centre of your view, aimed with your head. Look at the cutout for about a second and a half to pick it up, or press the viewer button if yours has one. Once it is stuck to your head, look where you want it and hold still for a moment to set it down. Five controls float underneath: nearer, farther, smaller, bigger, and lock, which stops the cutout being picked up by accident once it is where you want it. The sliders below are only the starting position.
